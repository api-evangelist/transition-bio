# Transition Bio

> Transition Bio is a preclinical drug discovery company built on biomolecular condensate science,
> a joint spin-out of the University of Cambridge and Harvard University with sites in Cambridge,
> Massachusetts and Cambridge, United Kingdom. It pairs droplet-microfluidics high-throughput
> molecular screening with a machine learning engine to find and optimize small molecules that
> modulate intrinsically disordered proteins and the condensates they form.

Important for agents: Transition Bio publishes NO public API. There is no developer portal, no API
